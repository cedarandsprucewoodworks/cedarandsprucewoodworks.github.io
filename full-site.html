Why We Use Cedar

Not All Wood Is
Created Equal

Cedar isn't just beautiful, it's the smartest choice for outdoor gardening. Here's why every Cedar & Spruce box starts and ends with real cedar.

🌿
Naturally Rot-Resistant

Cedar contains natural oils and tannins that resist fungi, moisture, and decay. No chemical treatments needed. Untreated pine may rot in 2–4 years; cedar thrives for 10–15.

🥕
100% Food-Safe

No pressure treatment, no chemical preservatives. Cedar is completely safe for growing vegetables, herbs, and edible plants, ideal for families with kids and pets.

🐛
Natural Insect Repellent

Cedar's natural aroma deters termites, carpenter ants, and wood-boring insects, so your boxes stay structurally sound season after season.

⚖️
Lightweight & Workable

Cedar is light compared to hardwoods or pressure-treated lumber, making it easy to move your planters before filling with soil.

Cedar Care Guide

Keep Your Box Thriving Year-Round

Cedar is naturally hardy. A little seasonal attention goes a long way.

🌱 Spring
🚿
Clean & Inspect

Rinse out last season's soil and debris. Check joints for any movement. Apply a coat of non-toxic wood sealant to refresh moisture protection before the wet season begins.

☀️ Summer
💧
Water & Monitor

Ensure drainage holes remain clear so water doesn't pool at the base. Cedar handles summer heat well. Watch for soil drying out faster in elevated planters and window boxes.

🍂 Fall
🍃
Prep for the Cold

Clear out finished plants and loosen compacted soil. For window boxes, inspect mounting hardware. Cedar will naturally silver-grey outdoors. This is beautiful and normal, not damage.

❄️ Winter
🏠
Store or Leave It

Smaller planter boxes can be stored indoors to extend their lifespan. Raised beds and larger window boxes can safely stay outside. Cedar handles freeze-thaw cycles well.
